## 3. Trend Signal Analysis

Agent infrastructure is the clear center of gravity today. The #1 trending repo, **prime-agent** (+2,642), is explicitly a "self-improving RLM agent" for long-running autonomous coding tasks, signaling that the community is moving beyond one-shot tool calling toward agents that learn from experience. The term "agent skills" has also become a formal category: **addyosmani/agent-skills** (+659) packages production-grade engineering skills, while **ECC** (239k stars) and **LifeOS** treat skills, memory, and harness design as first-class concerns.

Context engineering is separating from classic RAG. **firecrawl** (+835, 165k total) now calls itself "the context API," **headroom** compresses tokens before LLM ingest, and **claude-mem** gives agents persistent memory across sessions. This points to a new infrastructure layer built specifically around agent context, not just retrieval.

A second major signal is the rise of vectorless, graph-based knowledge. **code-graph-rag** (+682) and **Graphify** (105k total) both use deterministic AST parsing and knowledge graphs instead of vector stores — a response to the industry's demand for explainable, low-hallucination retrieval in large codebases.

Finally, open-weight models continue to drive tooling. **ollama** now markets itself around Kimi-K2.6, GLM-5.2, MiniMax, DeepSeek, and Qwen, confirming a multi-model world. And with **weathernext**, **TradingAgents**, and **RuView**, AI open source is visibly expanding from chat/coding into science, finance, and physical sensing.
