The community is now solving context persistence and skill distribution as the next bottleneck after model quality.

On the RAG/knowledge side, `Graphify` and `PageIndex` are pushing beyond dense-vector search toward knowledge graphs and “vectorless” reasoning, while `claude-mem` and `mem0` make memory a first-class citizen.

Corporate and cloud labs are increasingly open-sourcing agent infrastructure: Tencent’s memory hub and ByteDance’s `deer-flow` signal that large players are betting on modular, self-hostable agent stacks. Combined with the expansion of local runtimes — Ollama now advertises Kimi-K2.6, GLM-5.2, gpt-oss, and more — the ecosystem is moving toward open, persistent, audio-capable, long-running agents.
