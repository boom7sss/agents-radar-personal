## 3. Trend Signal Analysis

Today's list shows that the center of gravity in open-source AI has shifted from raw model weights to the **agent layer around them**. The explosive growth of [diagram-design](https://github.com/cathrynlavery/diagram-design), [anthropics/skills](https://github.com/anthropics/skills), and [obsidian-skills](https://github.com/kepano/obsidian-skills) points to a new packaging unit: small, composable, executable skills that teach coding agents like Claude Code to produce specialized outputs. This is a departure from prompt-only repositories — skills are versioned, tool-aware, and increasingly tied to real workflows.

A second strong signal is **context and cost engineering**. Projects like [headroom](https://github.com/headroomlabs-ai/headroom), [claude-mem](https://github.com/thedotmack/claude-mem), and [Switchyard](https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Switchyard) attack the same bottleneck: frontier LLM context windows and API spend. This aligns with the local-model wave in [unsloth](https://github.com/unslothai/unsloth), which now supports Qwen3.8, Kimi K3, Gemma 4, and DeepSeek-V4 — more models means more routing and memory infrastructure.

New directions are also emerging. [semantica](https://github.com/semantica-agi/semantica) brings graph-native infrastructure for accountable AI, [Graphify](https://github.com/Graphify-Labs/graphify) converts codebases into deterministic knowledge graphs, and [PageIndex](https://github.com/VectifyAI/PageIndex) advertises vectorless, reasoning-based RAG. These challenge the vector-database-centric RAG paradigm. Meanwhile, on-device AI is becoming practical: [needle](https://github.com/cactus-compute/needle) and [FluidVoice](https://github.com/altic-dev/FluidVoice) show that privacy-preserving, low-latency inference is moving from research into real products. Overall, developers are prioritizing **control, context, and composability** over raw model size — a sign of a maturing ecosystem.
