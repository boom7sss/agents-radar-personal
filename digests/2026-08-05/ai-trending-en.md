## 3. Trend Signal Analysis

The clearest signal is the rapid emergence of **agent middleware**: memory hubs, skill routers, and security layers are being productized around coding agents. [TencentDB-Agent-Memory](https://github.com/TencentCloud/TencentDB-Agent-Memory) and [claude-mem](https://github.com/thedotmack/claude-mem) show that persistent context is now a first-class infrastructure problem. [reverse-skill](https://github.com/zhaoxuya520/reverse-skill) (+2,297) and [obra/superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers) (+653) indicate that "skills" are becoming the new plugin format for Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, and other AI coding clients. Meanwhile, the +2,540 stars for [firecrawl/pdf-inspector](https://github.com/firecrawl/pdf-inspector) suggests that document preprocessing — making PDFs and pages AI-ready — is a hot bottleneck.

**Agent security** also appeared as a distinct new category: [uber/ADR](https://github.com/uber/ADR) brings observability, security benchmarking, and threat detection to enterprise AI agents. This signals movement from experimental agents to production deployments.

Efficiency remains essential. [AirLLM](https://github.com/lyogavin/airllm) on 4GB GPUs, [picollm](https://github.com/Picovoice/picollm) on-device inference, and [tiny-llm](https://github.com/skyzh/tiny-llm) all target lower-cost inference. [DeepSeek-Reasonix](https://github.com/esengine/DeepSeek-Reasonix) introduces prefix-cache-stable agent execution, directly addressing expensive context reloading.

Finally, "vectorless RAG" is gaining traction. [Graphify](https://github.com/Graphify-Labs/graphify), [PageIndex](https://github.com/VectifyAI/PageIndex), and [LEANN](https://github.com/StarTrail-org/LEANN) show that retrieval is no longer synonymous with vector databases — graphs, reasoning, and compression are becoming credible alternatives.
